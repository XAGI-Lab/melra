The AGPL-3.0, the Apache License 2.0 and the commercial license cover the code.
None of them gives you any right to use the MELRA name or logo.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to MELRA in plain text, factually: "works with MELRA", "a fork of
  MELRA", "built on MELRA", "an alternative to MELRA", or an article, talk or
  review about it.
- Redistribute an official, unmodified MELRA release under its own name.
- Link to the project.

## What needs a new name

A fork, or any modified build, may not use the MELRA name or logo, or a name or
logo that could be confused with them, for its product, service, package,
container image, binary or domain. Rename it, and remove the logo, before you
publish or offer it. You may still say, in plain text, that it is a fork of
MELRA or derived from it.

Do not use the name or logo in a way that suggests XAGI Labs made, endorses or
supports something it did not.
